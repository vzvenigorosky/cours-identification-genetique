# Dossier complet simplifié / Simplified full case

Entry point: `ex_dossier.html` (linked as a prototype from `exercices.html`). Logic and data: `assets/data/dossier.js` (pure ES module shared by the browser and Node). UI: `assets/dossier-ui.js`. Tests: `tools/dossier-selftest.mjs` (`npm run dossier:test`, in PR CI).

## Review status

Prototype for the maintainer's scientific review. Package contents and prices are those specified by the maintainer. Everything else (concentrations, degradation, thresholds, mutation allowance, ancestry and phenotype model, texts) is a **teaching assumption drafted by Claude**, not a validated laboratory protocol.

## Flow (no score anywhere)

1. **Requisition**, clear or vague. « Demander des précisions » works on any requisition: if it was vague the requester's answer is shown; if it was already clear a blue information box says so (never an error). « Proposer un devis » moves on.
2. **Quote**, one proposal per scellé (or none): Trace 100 €, Référence 50 €, « Trace — autosomaux + STR-Y » 100 €, « Référence — autosomaux + STR-Y » 50 €, « Trace — autosomaux + mtDNA » 600 €, « Référence — autosomaux + mtDNA » 400 €, Orientation 400 € (contents as specified). The Y and mtDNA names say that the autosomal STR profile(s) are included (#88). A muscle / organ fragment is quoted as a **Trace** (two extractions), not as a Référence, which is for a reference sample already taken such as a cheek swab (#86). « Vérifier » shows amber warnings: scellé not covered, scellé not needed (with the reason), Trace vs Référence mismatch, lineage marker missing / unnecessary / wrong lineage, Orientation mismatch. If the requisition was vague and nothing was asked, the warnings say they rely on the full information.
3. **Sampling**: the quote fixes the number of extractions per scellé; for each, choose the area (target, peripheral, or blank control). Warnings: no/unused extraction, blank area, peripheral area, target area never sampled.
4. **Quantification**: autosomal DNA (ng/µL), male DNA, degradation index, mtDNA copies.
5. **Amplification**: choose the extract of each analysis the quote paid for; the same extract may serve two profiles. Warnings: no extract, unassigned, very low concentration, a clearly better extract exists, no male DNA for a Y analysis (information: same extract used twice).
6. **Results**: autosomal profiles (columns per profile, `—` untyped, `a, *` single allele that may hide drop-out), Y / mtDNA haplotypes as **a code plus a quality percentage** (markers amplified, or share of the sequence), NGS ancestry / phenotype probabilities. Tools: kinship (LR for same person, parent–child, full siblings, second degree; haplotype comparison), consensus of replicates **built by the student** (tick replicates, add a column, then choose allele 1 / allele 2 at each locus among the alleles observed; no automatic consensus, #92), random match probability. No conclusion box; « Révéler la réponse » shows what could be concluded, with the student's results and with complete profiles.

## Low quantities of DNA (#106)

Profiles from poor extracts are **erratic and partial**, not all-or-nothing. The exercise simulates this with an **opt-in stochastic model** in `assets/epg/simulate.js` (`opts.stochastic`, off by default, so the EPG lab and its tests are unchanged): each allele peak is `K × N`, with `N ~ Poisson(expected / K)` the number of template copies amplified, so the variance grows as the template falls (CV = 1/√copies) and an allele can drop out; each locus also gets a log-normal amplification efficiency shared by its two alleles. Parameters (teaching values, calibrated against the owner's target table): `rfuPerNg` 10,000, `rfuPerCopy` (K) 130, `locusSigma` 0.6, stochastic threshold ST **2,400 RFU** (analytical threshold 150).

Median typed loci out of 21 (clean extract, DI 1.5, 300 simulated cases; owner target in brackets):

| ng in PCR | 1 | 0.5 | 0.25 | 0.1 | 0.05 | 0.025 | 0.01 | 0.005 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| result | 21 | 21 | 20 | 15 | 10 | 4 | 1 | 0 |
| target | 21 | 21 | 20–21 | 14–18 | 8–12 | 3–7 | 0–2 | 0 |

(p10–p90 at 0.1 ng: 13–18; at 0.05 ng: 7–12.) The ng in PCR is 10 µL of extract capped at 1 ng. Before this change a clean extract went from 0 to 21/21 loci between 0.002 and 0.005 ng/µL. The concentration ranges of trace-type sites were lowered (median target extract 0.019 ng/µL instead of 0.038; 20 % below 0.005); references, tissue and the sperm-bearing stain stay rich. With the best of two extracts, trace profiles now type 21 loci in about 19 % of cases, 16–20 in 45 %, 11–15 in 18 %, 6–10 in 12 % and 1–5 in 6 %. Two PCR replicates of the same low-quantity extract differ in most cases, which is what makes the consensus tool meaningful. Y-STR quality falls with the male DNA too (median 100 % at ≥ 0.1 ng, 64 % at 0.025 ng, 32 % at 0.01 ng).

**Why the stochastic threshold is 2,400 RFU.** A lone tall peak is read as a homozygote only above ST. With the new model a heterozygote can lose one allele, and with the owner's rule "one allelic exclusion makes PO exactly zero" that would wrongly exclude true parent–offspring pairs. Measured on truly parent–offspring pairs (≥ 8 comparable loci, all quantities and degradation levels): ST 400 → **17 %** falsely excluded; ST 1,600 → 0.13 % (7 / 5,211, always a heterozygote read as one tall peak, mostly with a large size gap between the alleles); **ST 2,400 → 1 / 5,088 (0.02 %)**, with every cell of the table still in range. The residual risk is a real feature of low-template work and is not hidden: a replicate would show it. Mutation is not simulated at autosomal loci.

## Consensus (student-built, #92)

The consensus tool no longer computes anything. The student ticks two or more replicate profiles and adds a **consensus column** (starts empty, 0/21 loci typed). In each cell, two drop-downs offer only the alleles actually observed in the chosen replicates: two alleles = genotype (the same twice = homozygote), one allele = partial (`a, *`), none = not typed. « Vérifier mon consensus » gives amber, ungraded hints. The column then feeds the kinship and frequency tools and the reveal like any other profile.

## Modelling choices and simplifications

- The requisition of every case with unidentified remains states their sex (« les restes sont ceux d’un homme / d’une femme non identifié(e) »), matching the simulated person (#87).
- **One source per sampling area; no mixtures.** A vaginal-swab-style mixture is out of scope; the exercise says so on the page.
- **Automatic differential extraction (#104, owner decision).** The student chooses nothing: no package, no price, no fraction. Where sperm is present or possible (the stained gusset of the underwear in `sa-ejaculation`, not when ejaculation is excluded) the quote stays a plain *Trace*, and the exhibit card (quote and sampling steps) and the sampling drop-down state that the analysis automatically becomes a differential extraction in which **only the sperm fraction is amplified**. Carry-over of the complainant's DNA into that fraction is **not simulated** (no allele of hers ever appears); it is only recalled as a margin warning — a **†** on the profiles from that area and an amber footnote under the profile table, repeated in the reveal — as a course reminder with nothing to handle. Sites that cannot carry sperm (waistband, clean control) are unaffected. Truth for "cannot say": the sperm is always present (unchanged). Assumption: the price of the quote is unchanged (the owner did not state otherwise); a mixture-reading exercise is **not** planned unless the owner asks again.
- Cases: `id-parent`, `id-uncle` (Y), `id-maternal-aunt` (mtDNA), `sa-ejaculation`, `misc-flat` reuse the réquisition texts, vague/clear variants and clarifications of the quote simulator (`devis.js`, `requisition-shared.js`); their expected package comes from `expectedLine`. `trace-suspect` and `orientation` are new. The 14-swab sexual-assault template (`sa-sites`) is not reused (too many scellés for a full run). Tariffs (garde à vue, urgence) do not exist here; deadline sentences are suppressed.
- Genetic truth: persons are built with the EPG genetics module (Mendelian autosomes, Y from the father with a 0.2 % one-step mutation, mtDNA from the mother). Profiles come from the EPG peak simulator (21 GlobalFiler autosomal loci, 25 Yfiler Plus markers), called at AT 150 / ST 2400 RFU (see *Low quantities of DNA*); a single allele below ST is shown as `a, *`.
- mtDNA: 24 variable sites in 8 amplicons; quality = amplicons obtained. Y: quality = markers fully typed. Haplotype codes are hashes of the typed values; comparison uses the markers typed in both.
- **Kinship levels (#95 #96 #97):** PO (parent–offspring), FS (full siblings) and HS/AV/GC (half-siblings / avuncular / grandparent–grandchild; same IBD probabilities, so indistinguishable with these markers). Per locus, pairwise IBD formula (k0, k1, k2) on loci typed in both profiles; frequencies from `assets/epg/frequencies.js` (approximate European). **No mutation allowance for the levels: one locus with no shared allele makes the PO LR and the PO posterior exactly 0.** Two non-PO relatives (uncle/nephew, aunt/nephew) show such exclusions in practice (100 % of complete profiles over 300 seeds; ~98–99 % of noisy simulated runs, the rest typed too few discriminating loci); true parent–offspring pairs showed none in 1,500 simulated comparisons. For each level the tool shows the **LR against unrelated and the posterior probability**, each level being tested **separately against unrelated** with equal priors of **50 % / 50 %** (owner decision, #99): posterior = LR / (1 + LR). The three probabilities are independent comparisons and do not add up to 100 %. **Arbitrary threshold (#100): a posterior of 99.99999 % (LR ≈ 10⁷ with 50 % priors) is the exercise's convention for treating a level as established**; it is stated under the table and in the reveal, which also lists the levels that reach it. It is a teaching convention, not a legal or laboratory standard. Percentages ≥ 99 % are shown with five decimals, and "≥ 99,99999 %" at or above the threshold, so that a value just under it is never displayed as 100 %. LR magnitude on complete 21-locus profiles (600 simulated pairs each, median log10 LR): PO 7.9 (78 % of pairs ≥ 10⁷), FS 6.4 (43 % ≥ 10⁷), HS / AV / GC about 1.8 (never ≥ 10⁷: second-degree relatives cannot reach that with 21 STRs); the allele-frequency model is unchanged (mean expected heterozygosity 0.83). A pair of identical profiles (replicates) is flagged: kinship levels do not apply to one person. The "same person" identity LR (used by the profile-match comparison) keeps a 0.2 % typing-error allowance. Random match = product of p² / 2pq over reliably typed loci, no θ correction. Lineage haplotype frequency is deliberately **not** computed (needs a counting database).
- Reveal wording: statements compare profiles only, use a verbal LR scale, and end with a fixed sentence saying nothing is inferred about the case. The test suite rejects assertive words (guilty, proves, certainty…).

## Owner decisions

- **Y-STR is intentionally free.** Trace-Y costs the same as Trace (100 €) and Référence-Y the same as Référence (50 €). Confirmed by the owner (2026-11-04); not a typo, not to be "fixed".

## Points still open for the maintainer

- Orientation replaces the STR profile (no STR is produced); the two Orientation cases state that no STR profile is requested.
- Concentration ranges, the 5 pg/µL indicative threshold, degradation-to-drop-out mapping and the ancestry / phenotype tables need review.
- A scellé takes exactly one package; adding a second package to the same scellé is not possible.
